## Safari bar tint follows the camera
- Problem: on iOS Safari (ikou-demo.vercel.app) the status-bar and URL-bar strips kept the color sampled at load
  (forest green / beach cream) while the camera moved.
- Fix: GameScene `tintEdges` prop. Each frame it takes the ground color at the scene's top row and bottom row
  (`groundColorAt`). When one changes, it colors two 4px fixed edge bars (what iOS 26 Safari samples),
  html / body background and a `theme-color` meta (older Safari). Cleared on unmount. On for IslandScene and
  the /MobDev island preview.
- Checked in the browser at 375x812: spawn green/cream, north brown/green, shore cream/ocean blue. Not checked on a real iPhone yet.

## Safari tint, take 2 (tested in the iOS 26 simulator)
- The edge bars did NOT work: Safari samples a fixed element at the screen edge once (when its solid strips
  appear) and never again; changing the bar color, hiding it 1 frame or 300 ms all left the strips frozen.
- Static CSS was not overriding anything (checked: islandPreview / Game layout <style> have no !important;
  the live colors are inline). The 40px debug bar did paint the new color; Safari just didn't re-read it.
- What works: no fixed element on the page. Then Safari paints its strips with the body background and
  follows changes live. GameScene tintEdges now sets html/body background (+ theme-color) to the ground at
  the screen's top edge; the edge bars are gone.
- /MobDev wrapper: position fixed inset 0 -> absolute 100lvh (like /Game's IslandScene), so the scene runs
  under the glass URL bar and that bar shows the scene itself.
- Limit: Safari uses ONE body color for both strips, so the thin strip under the URL bar shows the top color.
- Simulator results: spawn, volcano (top brown), shore (top cream, ocean through the URL bar) all correct.
